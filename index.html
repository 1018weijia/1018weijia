Biography

I am a Master's student in Computer Technology at the School of Computer Science, Sun Yat-sen University. I received my B.Eng. in Intelligent Science and Technology from the School of Intelligent Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University in June 2025. I am passionate about embodied intelligence and robotics. I am familiar with Linux, proficient in Python, and experienced with simulation software such as NVIDIA Isaac Sim.

My research interests lie in Embodied Intelligence, especially vision-language-action models for robotic manipulation. My work includes recovery-driven policy optimization, scalable data generation for bimanual manipulation, and open-ended simulation for embodied AI.

Feel free to contact me by email if you are interested in discussing or collaborating with me.
